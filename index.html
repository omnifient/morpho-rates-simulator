Underlying Markets

Model: Morpho AdaptiveCurveIrm. Target utilization 90%, curve steepness 4, adjustment speed 50/yr, rate at target clamped to [0.1%, 200%] APR. At 100% utilization the rate at target doubles about every 5 days, and at 95% about every 10 days. Below target it decays symmetrically in normalized error (45% utilization halves it every ~10 days). The simulation steps hourly and accrues interest on both borrow and supply, so utilization drifts upward unless that option is off. Supply APY is borrow APY × utilization × (1 − market fee). Vault deposit APY is the position-weighted supply APY × (1 − performance fee) − management fee. Markets whose IRM is not adaptive are held at their current rate. Actions that would revert on-chain (borrows or withdrawals past liquidity, V1 deposits past caps) are skipped, not partly applied. Vault deposits and withdrawals are applied before borrows. "Effective" APYs compound the simulated rate path over the period. V2 deposits must fit the caps on the liquidity adapter (adapter, collateral and market; relative caps use pre-deposit total assets). V2 total assets grow at most at the vault's max rate, so deposit APY is capped by it and any excess builds a buffer that pays out later. Reward APRs are modelled as fixed yearly emissions at constant token prices, so they dilute as supply, borrow or vault assets grow. Net APY follows Morpho's convention: APY + supply reward APR, or APY − borrow reward APR. Vault rewards are vault-level programs only; market rewards earned by the vault's positions aren't assumed to reach depositors. Estimates only, not financial advice.
